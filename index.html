Before you come in

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome. For bridal makeup, hair smoothening, colouring or anything that takes over an hour, booking makes sure an artist is free for you.

Do you offer beauty parlour home service in Trichy?

Yes. Choose "At my home" when booking and add your area. Bridal makeup and facials are the most common home bookings across Trichy.

Which parts of Trichy are close to the studio?

We're on Kattabomman Street in Kumaran Nagar, a short ride from Woraiyur, Thillai Nagar, Vayalur Road and Puthur. For home service we travel across Trichy.

Is Spin a ladies beauty parlour?

Yes, the studio serves women. Groom makeup is available for weddings; mention it in your booking and we'll arrange it.

Can I rent the bridal outfit and jadai too?

Yes. We rent lehengas, gowns and bridal wear, and make flower jadai to match your saree. Book a fitting along with your makeup trial so everything is matched.

Do the beautician courses come with a certificate?

Yes. Spin Academy is an approved training centre, and the beautician courses come with a Central Government certificate. Workshops and master classes include a certificate of participation.

How long are the courses, and what do they cost?

It depends on the course and batch. Pick the course in the enquiry form and we'll send you the duration, fees and next start date.
